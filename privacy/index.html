Privacy Policy

Englishالعربية

Daily Feed Automation and Gamex Automation use Meta’s Page management permissions to manage and publish our own content on the Daily Feed and Gamex pages. Only authorized team members operate the apps.

We do not ask members of the public to sign in to these apps or collect personal data from them through the apps. We may use aggregate Page performance metrics to assess our content.

For a privacy question, message the relevant official Page on Facebook.

  • Gamex on Facebook
